# Student-facing disclosure — draft, pending school approval

## Your writing, your responsibility

This writing space helps you produce your assessed work independently and helps your teacher understand how your document developed. Unless your teacher has explicitly approved it for this task, you must not use an AI assistant to write or substantially rewrite your essay. Cite sources and follow your school's academic-integrity policy.

InkWitness saves your draft on this device while you write. It records the document's editing history and selected events such as pasted passages, substantial changes, and recovery from a previous session. Those records are included in the Word document you export. Teachers can inspect them using a local viewer. They are process observations, not automatic cheating decisions.

**Your essay is not sent by this website to a plagiarism company or AI detector.** The editor runs locally in your browser after its site assets load. You submit the DOCX separately through your school's approved assignment system. Browser storage is not a guaranteed backup: keep your Word file or an optional recovery copy.

[Read Ikast-Brande Gymnasium's IB academic integrity policy](https://www.ikast-gym.dk/ib/more-about-the-ib/policies)

Mandatory when starting a new document: student full name, assessment title. The editor adds them to the Word document header, properties and optional faint watermark. On first deliberate Word export, you will be asked to confirm your work follows the stated rules. This is recorded as a declaration, not a digital signature.
